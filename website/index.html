View exact word counts and method

Counts include every occurrence in event narratives across all matching records, including records beyond the first page. Words are lowercased; punctuation, numbers, and words shorter than three letters are removed. Word forms such as “flood” and “flooding” stay separate. Broader episode context is excluded. Frequency describes language, not storm severity.
